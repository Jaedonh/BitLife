# LifeLoop Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

LifeLoop ("the app") is a life-simulation game published by **[YOUR LEGAL NAME OR COMPANY]** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Host this page at a public URL (for example GitHub Pages) and enter that URL in App Store Connect and Google Play Console; it must also be reachable from inside the app (Settings → Privacy policy).

## Summary

- The game runs **entirely on your device**. Your saved lives, settings and graveyard are stored locally and are never uploaded by us.
- We do **not** require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
- The free version shows **advertising** provided by Google AdMob. The ads SDK collects device identifiers and usage data as described below.
- **In-app purchases** are processed by Apple or Google; purchase state is validated by RevenueCat.
- The app is **not directed to children under 13**, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Information collected by third-party SDKs

### Google AdMob (advertising) — free version only

To show ads, Google Mobile Ads may collect:

- Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA, only if you allow tracking), and app-instance identifiers.
- Ad interaction and usage data: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped, and coarse diagnostic data about the device (model, OS version, language, approximate location derived from IP address).

How it is used: to serve, measure and limit the frequency of ads, to detect fraud, and, where you have consented, to personalise ads. Google's handling of this data is described at <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads> and <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

Your choices:

- **iOS:** the App Tracking Transparency prompt lets you deny tracking; ads will then be non-personalised. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
- **EEA, UK and Switzerland, and US states with privacy laws:** a consent form is shown on first launch, and you can change your choices at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options.
- **Premium:** purchasing LifeLoop Premium removes all advertising; the ads SDK is then not initialised.

### RevenueCat (purchases)

When you open the Premium screen or make a purchase, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated on your device, the device vendor identifier (IDFV) or Android app-set ID, and your purchase history for this app, in order to validate receipts and restore purchases across reinstalls. RevenueCat's privacy policy: <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>.

### Apple App Store / Google Play

Purchases are completed by the store. We never see your payment details. The stores may also send us aggregated, anonymised crash and performance statistics.

## Information we store on your device

- Game saves (your current life and past lives), settings, and ad-frequency counters, stored in the app's private storage.
- The premium unlock flag.
- Unlocked achievements, each with the name and age of the life that unlocked it and the date.

This data stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. You can delete your current life from Settings → Delete current life, every past life (the graveyard, including the names you typed and the generated obituaries) from Graveyard → Clear graveyard, and your achievements (with the names stored next to them) from Settings → Reset achievements.

## Data retention

We hold no personal data ourselves. Ad and purchase data is retained by Google and RevenueCat according to their policies linked above (Google ad-serving logs are typically anonymised after 9 months and deleted after 18 months).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data, and to object to or withdraw consent for personalised advertising. Because we do not hold personal data, requests about advertising data should be directed to Google via the links above; you can also withdraw consent at any time in the app. For any other request, contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

## Children

LifeLoop is rated for ages 13 and over (Teen). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The ads SDK is configured to request age-appropriate (Teen) ad content only. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

The app transmits data only to the services named above, over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
