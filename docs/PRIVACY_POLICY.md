# Yearborn Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

Yearborn ("the app") is a text life-simulation game for iPhone, iPad and Android, published by **[YOUR LEGAL NAME]** ("we", "us"). We are responsible for how the app handles personal information (the "controller" under European law). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and the choices you have. Questions or requests: **[CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## The short version

- **No account.** We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
- **Your game stays on your device.** Saves, settings, past lives and achievements are stored only on your phone or tablet. We never receive them.
- **Ads in the free version come from Google AdMob**, which collects device identifiers and ad data, as described below. You can limit this, and Premium removes ads entirely.
- **Purchases go through Apple or Google.** RevenueCat checks them for us using an anonymous ID, each time the app starts. We never see your payment details.
- **No analytics of our own.** We do not use any analytics or tracking service besides the ads described here, and we do not sell personal information for money.
- **Rated 13+.** The app is not meant for children under 13.

## 1. What stays on your device

The app keeps these in its private storage on your device:

- your current life (the saved game), including any names you type;
- the graveyard: up to 200 past lives with their obituaries, and your ribbon collection;
- unlocked achievements, each with the name and age of the life that unlocked it and the date;
- your settings, whether Premium is unlocked, and counters that limit how often ads appear.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. If you tap **Share** on a tombstone, the obituary text goes only to the app you pick in your phone's share menu.

You can delete this data from inside the app: **Settings → Delete current life**, **Graveyard → Clear graveyard** (every past life, with its names and obituary) and **Settings → Reset achievements** (with the names stored next to them). Uninstalling the app deletes all of it from the device (see section 9 about phone backups).

## 2. Advertising in the free version (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland). Each time the free version starts, Google's consent tool first asks Google whether your region needs a consent message and shows it if so; then, if ads are allowed, the app starts Google's Mobile Ads SDK. Neither runs once Premium is active. The Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects:

- **Device identifiers:** the advertising ID (Android advertising ID; on iPhone and iPad the IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and app-set or app-instance IDs.
- **Your IP address**, which may be used to estimate your general location (such as your city or region; never your precise location).
- **App and ad interactions:** such as app launches and taps, and which ads were shown, viewed or tapped.
- **Diagnostic and performance information:** such as app launch time, freezes, crashes and energy use, device model, operating system version and language.

Google uses this to show ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ads and how the app is used (analytics), to find and fix problems, to prevent fraud and keep its services secure and, only where you allow it, to personalize ads to your interests. Google also uses some of this data for its own purposes, under its own policies: [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

For every ad request, the app limits ads to content suitable for teens. It asks for non-personalized ads when you refuse personalized ads in Google's consent form or do not allow tracking on iPhone or iPad, and an opt-out you make in Google's consent tool under US state law is passed to Google with your ad requests.

### Your ad choices

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** on first launch, Google's consent form asks whether Google and its ad partners may use your data. If you do not agree, the app shows only non-personalized (limited) ads, or none. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options**.
- **US states with privacy laws** (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and Oregon): you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising in **Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options** where it is shown, with the phone settings below, or by emailing us. See section 7.
- **iPhone and iPad:** after Google's consent step, the app asks for permission to track you (Apple's App Tracking Transparency), unless you already refused in Google's consent form. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and the app asks only for non-personalized ads. You can change this later in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.
- **Android:** you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings (**Settings → Privacy → Ads**, or **Settings → Google → Ads** on some phones). Google then cannot use it to personalize ads.
- **Premium:** buying Yearborn Premium removes all ads. Once Premium is active, the app does not start the ads service at all.
- **Report an ad:** if an ad is inappropriate, tap **Settings → Privacy → Report an ad**. It opens an email to us (see section 4).

## 3. Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

Yearborn Premium is a one-time purchase made through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment under their own privacy policies; we never see your name, card or account details.

We use **RevenueCat** (RevenueCat, Inc.) to check purchases and to make **Restore purchases** work after a reinstall or on a new device. Each time the app starts, it asks RevenueCat whether Premium is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID that it creates for your installation of the app, the store's receipt or purchase token, which product was bought, when, its price and currency, and the technical details needed to answer (such as app and system version, store country and IP address). RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf, as our service provider. Its privacy policy: [revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

The stores also give us sales reports and, if you have agreed to share them with developers, crash and performance statistics. These come as totals or without your name, and we use them only to fix problems and run the business.

## 4. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and to fix what you reported. **Report an ad** and **Contact** in Settings open your own email app; Report an ad fills in the app version and whether you use iOS or Android, and never adds an identifier. Nothing is sent until you send the email.

## 5. Why we use information (legal bases)

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the legal bases are:

- **Personalized ads:** your consent, given in Google's consent form. You can withdraw it at any time (section 2).
- **Non-personalized ads and fraud prevention:** our legitimate interest in keeping the game free by showing ads, and your consent where the law requires it.
- **Purchases and restores:** to provide what you bought (our contract with you).
- **Support emails:** our legitimate interest in answering you.

## 6. How long information is kept

- **On your device:** until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
- **Google (ads):** under Google's own retention rules. Google says it anonymizes advertising data in its server logs by removing part of the IP address after 9 months and cookie information after 18 months.
- **RevenueCat (purchases):** for as long as we offer the app, so that Restore purchases keeps working, or until you ask us to delete it.
- **Support emails:** for as long as we need them to help you, and no longer than 12 months after the conversation ends.

## 7. US state privacy rights

The free version makes these categories of personal information available to Google for advertising: **identifiers** (advertising ID, app-instance IDs, IP address), **internet or other network activity** (app and ad interactions), **approximate location** (estimated from the IP address) and **diagnostic information**. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app ID and **commercial information** (purchase history) as our service provider. We do not collect sensitive personal information.

Some US state laws call letting an ad network use device identifiers for personalized ads a "sale" of personal information or "sharing" for "targeted advertising". In that sense only, the free version may sell or share the categories above with Google. We do not sell personal information for money.

Depending on your state, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected about you and to access, correct or delete it; to opt out of its sale, its sharing and targeted advertising (section 2 explains how); and to appeal if we turn down a request (reply to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject). You can make a request yourself or through an authorized agent. We will not treat you differently for using these rights: the game and the price of Premium are the same for everyone.

The app does not read web browsers' "Do Not Track" signals; use the ad choices in section 2.

## 8. Your rights everywhere else

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA, the UK GDPR, Brazil's LGPD or Canada's PIPEDA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

**How to make a request:** email **[CONTACT EMAIL]**. We will answer within 30 days. Because the app has no accounts, we do not know who you are: game data on your device is deleted by you (section 1), and requests about ads data are best made to Google, which holds it, through the links in section 2. To find a purchase record at RevenueCat, include the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt.

## 9. Deleting your data

- **Game data:** use the in-app options in section 1, or uninstall the app. If you back up your phone (iCloud on iPhone and iPad, or Google backup on Android), your backup may also contain a copy of the app's data. That copy is stored in your own account; we cannot see it, and you control it in your phone's backup settings.
- **Ads data:** reset or delete your advertising ID (section 2) and use Google's controls.
- **Purchase record at RevenueCat or support emails:** email **[CONTACT EMAIL]**, with the store order number for a purchase record. Your purchase itself stays in your Apple or Google account, so Restore purchases still works later.
