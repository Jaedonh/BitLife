Restore your Premium purchase

Premium is a one-time purchase that belongs to your Apple ID or Google account, so you can restore it for free after reinstalling the app or on a new phone or tablet.

  1. Sign in to the App Store or Google Play with the same account you used to buy Premium.
  2. Make sure you are connected to the internet.
  3. Open Yearborn and go to Settings → Premium → Restore purchases. You can also tap Restore purchases under Unlock Premium on the Premium screen.

I bought Premium but still see ads

Tap Restore purchases, then close and reopen the app. If that does not help, email us the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt and we will look into it.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you paid. For the App Store, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, see Google Play's refund help page.

Saves and your data

Where is my game saved?

Only on your device. Yearborn has no account and no cloud save, and we never receive your game.

Does deleting the app delete my data?

Yes. Uninstalling Yearborn deletes your current life, your graveyard, your achievements and your settings from the device. If you back up your phone (iCloud, or Google backup on Android), that backup may still hold a copy in your own account until the backup is replaced. Your Premium purchase is not lost: restore it any time.

How do I delete my data without deleting the app?

  • Settings → Delete current life removes the life you are playing.
  • Graveyard → Clear graveyard removes every past life, with its names and obituary.
  • Settings → Reset achievements locks every achievement again and removes the names stored with them.

To ask us to delete a purchase record or an email you sent us, write to the address above. The privacy policy explains what the ads and purchase services keep.

Can I move my game to a new phone?

There is no cloud save. A full phone-to-phone transfer or a restore from a phone backup usually brings the app's data along, but it is not guaranteed. Premium always comes back with Restore purchases.

Ads and ad privacy

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

  • In the app: Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options. This button appears in regions where Google's consent choices apply, such as the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws.
  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, then turn Yearborn off. Ads will no longer be personalized.
  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads), then delete or reset your advertising ID.

How often will I see ads?

A small banner on the main screen, and at most one full-screen ad every six years you play: never in your first twelve years, never more than three in one session, and never over a choice. Video ads that give a reward are always optional. Premium removes all ads.

How do I report an inappropriate ad?

In the app, tap Settings → Privacy → Report an ad. It opens an email to us with a short form: what the ad showed, where it appeared (banner, full-screen ad or rewarded video) and roughly when. A screenshot helps. You can also write to the address above. We will block the ad in our ad settings. Many ads also have a small AdChoices icon with its own feedback option.

Other questions

Does Yearborn need the internet?

No. The game plays offline. Ads, buying Premium and restoring it need a connection.

Is Yearborn suitable for children?

Yearborn is rated 13+ (Teen). Lives can include dating, crime, prison, alcohol and death, all in text and with consequences. There is no gambling, no chat and no content from other players.

I found a typo or a sentence that makes no sense

Please email us the sentence and roughly what happened before it. Every event is hand-written, and we fix these in updates.
